LUNAVIA Support

The road to the Moon — and back.

LUNAVIA is a lunar mission game for iPhone by LAJU PRODUCTIONS: launch, descent and landing, lift-off, docking and the return home.

Contact

Questions, problems or feedback: lajuproductions@gmail.com

Please tell us your iPhone model, its iOS version and the BUILD shown on the game’s first screen.

Common questions

Do I need an account or an internet connection? No. The game works offline and has no account.

Where is my progress saved? On your iPhone only. Deleting the game deletes its progress from the device; iPhone backups may still hold a copy.

Is the Hall of Fame online? No. It is local to your iPhone, and your pilot name is never uploaded.
