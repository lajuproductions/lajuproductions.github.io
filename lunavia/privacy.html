Privacy Policy

Effective: 23 November 2026

Português (Brasil)

LUNAVIA does not collect your personal information. Everything the game keeps stays on your iPhone.

Who we are

LUNAVIA is made and published by LAJU PRODUCTIONS, an independent game studio, which is responsible for this policy. You can contact us at lajuproductions@gmail.com.

What the game does not do

  • It has no account and no sign-in. It never asks for your name, e-mail address or other personal details.
  • It does not connect to the internet while you play. Everything it needs is installed with it.
  • It contains no advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting code of its own, and no third-party service that collects data.
  • It does not use your camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos.

What stays on your iPhone

The game saves your progress on your device only: missions, records, medals, achievements, training results, your settings (sound, subtitles, language) and the Hall of Fame. It keeps a second copy in its own storage on the iPhone so that an update cannot lose your progress. None of this is sent to us or to anyone else.

Hall of Fame

The Hall of Fame is local to your iPhone. The pilot name you type is saved on the device and is never uploaded. There is no online or worldwide leaderboard.

Apple and your device

Backups that your iPhone makes to iCloud or to a computer can include the game’s saved progress. Those backups belong to your Apple account and are covered by Apple’s privacy policy.

If you choose to share analytics with app developers (Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements), Apple may give us crash reports and usage statistics about LUNAVIA. Apple decides what these contain, under its own privacy policy. You can turn this off at any time in the same setting.

Deleting your data

The game has no button that erases your progress. Deleting LUNAVIA from your iPhone removes everything it saved on the device. Copies inside existing iCloud or computer backups remain until those backups are deleted or replaced. You can change your Hall of Fame name at any time on the game’s Hall of Fame page.

Because we do not receive any of your game data, we hold no game data about you that we could show, correct or delete. If you have a question, write to us.

When you write to us

If you e-mail us, we receive your e-mail address and your message. We use them only to reply and to look into what you describe. We do not share them or use them for marketing, and you can ask us to delete your messages at any time. E-mail to lajuproductions@gmail.com is handled by Google (Gmail) under its own privacy policy.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

This website

This website has no cookies, analytics or trackers. It is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which may record technical information, such as your IP address and the time of your visit, under GitHub’s own privacy policy.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version here with a new effective date, and the game will carry it in an update.
